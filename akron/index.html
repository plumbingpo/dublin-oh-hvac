How Akron Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

A little routine care makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that breaks down in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, central air typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like rising energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and causes far less stress than an emergency swap during the worst heat wave of summer.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A weak capacitor costs little to replace, yet the same part, ignored for a season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so help is one call away as soon as something sounds or feels wrong in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
